## How it works

Domain control, reachable IP, PTR authority, port policy, reputation and provider terms.

Public mail needs more than software: a suitable stable address, domain records, PTR authority, reachable/allowed SMTP, TLS, reputation and ongoing operation. Providers can block SMTP and an HTTP tunnel does not replace public MTA transport. Document a go/no-go decision before exposing a mail service or buying resources.
