## How it works

Retries, bounces, throttling, blocklists, reputation, relay options and monitoring.

Mail can be accepted locally yet remain queued or be rejected downstream. Queue IDs, status codes and timestamps locate the failure. Retries and bounces need bounded policies, and sending more copies is not a fix for a configuration or reputation problem. Inspect only synthetic lab mail and preserve selective controls.
