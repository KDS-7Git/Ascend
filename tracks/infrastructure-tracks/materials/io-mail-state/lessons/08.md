# 08. Recover a mail service

Recover the local mail service from a fresh failure and document any public-service dependencies.

Author estimate: 90 minutes guided practice and 120 minutes independent work; split into sessions as needed. The portable preparation alone is usually 5–15 minutes. Setup, downloads and provider approval are separate.

## How it works

Secrets, independent restore, relay controls, retained data and operating cost.

Recovery combines data, service configuration, identities and access policy. Restoring old configuration can revive an obsolete credential or open relay rule. Verify legitimate mail delivery and mailbox access again, reject old credentials and unauthorized relay, and document which parts of public readiness remain external dependencies.
