## How it works

Tailscale, Headscale, coordination, direct data paths, NAT traversal and relays.

A coordination service distributes identity and peer information; the actual encrypted data path can be direct or relayed. Tailscale and a self-hosted Headscale coordinator share useful concepts but differ in operating responsibilities and supported versions. A hosted control plane does not imply every peer is directly reachable.
