## How it works

Queued operations, states, locking, host observations, reconciliation and failed jobs.

Provisioning is an operation with intermediate states. Requested, configured-next-boot and guest-observed resources can differ. Keep reservations until cleanup succeeds, serialize conflicting operations and expose errors rather than turning the UI green after a button click. Quota rejection should not partially allocate another guest.

