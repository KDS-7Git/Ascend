## How it works

Public/private addresses, router forwarding, CGNAT, IPv6, DNS and continuous operation.

An always-on origin requires power, operating-system startup, service startup and network reachability. A VPS supplies a provider-managed host boundary, but the guest still needs updates, access policy and a recoverable release. Home NAT, CGNAT and IPv6 decide whether an inbound route can exist. An external client is necessary to establish internet reachability.
