## How it works

Outbound connector, Quick versus named tunnels, public HTTP routes, Access and restart.

cloudflared establishes an outbound connection between a running origin and a relay service. A Quick Tunnel supplies a temporary public HTTP hostname; a stable named route has account/domain/configuration requirements. The connector does not keep a stopped origin alive, and routing alone is not an authentication policy. Outbound 7844 UDP/TCP restrictions can prevent connection.
