## How it works

Log rotation, journal quotas, metrics cardinality, retention and disk headroom.

Logs and metrics are useful only while their storage cost remains bounded. Cardinality, retention, rotation, WAL and compaction all consume resources. Deleting all logs conceals the incident instead of controlling growth. Apply limits only to the disposable lab service or guest and keep enough reserve to recover.
