## How it works

Backup consistency, independent copies, restore drills, RPO/RTO and retention.

Backup success means the required data can be restored independently with an understood loss window and recovery time. A snapshot on the same disk shares that disk's failure. A cold or application-consistent backup has a defined consistency point. Restore into a new target before trusting the procedure.
