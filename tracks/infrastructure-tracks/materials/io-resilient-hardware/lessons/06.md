## How it works

cgroups, CPU weight/quota, memory high/max, OOM, I/O and reservations.

CPU weight controls relative scheduling, while CPU quota caps bandwidth. memory.high triggers pressure/reclaim and memory.max can lead to an OOM boundary. These do not reserve invisible extra resources. Budget aggregate guests, host services, caches and transient operations, and apply experiments only to owned scopes.
