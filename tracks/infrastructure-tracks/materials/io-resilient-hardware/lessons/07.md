## How it works

DNS/tunnel expiry, WAN loss, credentials, restart policy and incident communication.

Network recovery should restore the intended route and identity rather than expose everything. Time, DNS, tunnel credentials, origin readiness and WAN reachability can fail independently. Detect the responsible boundary, keep saved state and repeat access-denial controls after restoring service.
