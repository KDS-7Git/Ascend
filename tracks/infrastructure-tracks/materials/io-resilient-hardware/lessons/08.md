## How it works

SSD/RAM upgrades, separate hosts, service placement and runbooks.

An upgrade should move the existing service and evidence rather than restart learning from scratch. A larger SSD may solve write/space constraints; more RAM may allow larger working sets; a second host changes failure domains. Migrate deliberately, retain rollback material and verify addresses, storage ownership, state and recovery on the new target.
