## How it works

Requests, users, arrival rate, concurrency, response sizes, latency distributions and SLOs.

A capacity statement needs a workload, an observation window and a success condition. Concurrent users, requests in progress and requests per second are different quantities. Include failed and unfinished requests in accounting, and examine tails rather than only average successful latency. Recorded CSV fixtures let you learn the calculation without generating external load.
