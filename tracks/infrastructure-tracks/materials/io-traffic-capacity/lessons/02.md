## How it works

Caddy/HAProxy/NGINX, balancing algorithms, active/passive checks, readiness and draining.

A balancer chooses an eligible backend according to its policy. Readiness should reflect whether that instance can accept the intended work; a process being alive is weaker evidence. The proxy itself may remain a single failure point. Configure the policy explicitly and distinguish new traffic removal from completion of already active work.
