## How it works

Bounded queues, semaphores, timeouts, pool limits, retries, cancellation and fairness.

An unbounded queue converts overload into growing memory and latency. Admission limits, bounded payloads, finite timeouts and retry budgets protect a service. Count rejected work explicitly and verify recovery after the burst. A lower latency achieved by discarding work is not increased useful throughput.
