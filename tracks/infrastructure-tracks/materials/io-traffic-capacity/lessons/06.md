## How it works

k6 open/closed models, coordinated omission, dropped iterations, warmup and generator limits.

A closed workload starts more work after previous work finishes, so offered load can fall when the server slows. An open arrival model schedules arrivals independently and can expose dropped iterations when the generator lacks capacity. Neither model alone is universal; report generator saturation and the workload shape.
