## How it works

CPU service time, memory working sets, disk capacity/IOPS, bandwidth and Little's Law.

Little's Law relates mean throughput, mean time and mean work in progress within the same stable boundary; it is not a worker-count formula. CPU service time differs from wall latency. Storage, uplink, transient memory and bounded peak work may dominate before CPU. Size from measured inputs and add explicit headroom.
